# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript, chosen for a lightweight static site that can be reviewed locally and later published through GitHub Pages without changing the architecture.

## Users

Decision makers, directors, founders, and organizational leaders who need to bring order, structure, and strategic clarity to their companies through management systems and improvement models.

## Product Purpose

Royand is a corporate consulting brand focused on designing personalized management models for each client. Its website must explain the firm clearly, communicate trust, and invite a conversation with a qualified contact.

## Positioning

Royand connects the idea of family legacy with organizational foundations: just as a legacy needs solid bases to grow and endure, a company needs structure, order, and direction to evolve and transcend.

## Operating Context

The site is a public corporate landing page in Spanish. Visitors should quickly understand what Royand offers, why its approach matters, what values guide the firm, and how to contact Alejandra González.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first release is a static corporate website with no backend deployment. It must be ready for a future GitHub remote and GitHub Pages publication. The site must not invent metrics, case studies, clients, certifications, or commercial claims not supplied in the brief.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Royand. The name comes from the final letters of Monroy and the first letters of Ander, and is also the name of the founder's son. The meaning is love, legacy, and the construction of something that transcends.

Slogan: “Orden que impulsa tu crecimiento”.

Voice: close, kind, professional, adult, trustworthy, clear, and oriented toward accompaniment.

Core palette: deep blue `#0C2333`, stone `#CBC3B6`, and terracotta `#B65D35`.

Manual typography references: Montserrat, Montaser Arabic, Manrope, Avenir Next Arabic, and Cocomat Pro. Web implementation may use practical equivalents when needed.

## Product Principles

1. Explain order, structure, and strategic alignment in concrete business language.
2. Express confidence without sounding cold or generic.
3. Use visual identity to communicate foundations, growth, clarity, and legacy.
4. Keep claims grounded in the supplied brand material.
5. Make contact clear and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must support semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, readable responsive typography, accessible forms, and reduced-motion preferences.
